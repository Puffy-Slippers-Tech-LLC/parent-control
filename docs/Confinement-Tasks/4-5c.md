# 4-5c — Interrupted finalization and durable admission barriers

Acceptance: installed
Targets: onpc-Ubuntu26.04, onpc-Fedora44
Requires: 4-5b

## Required result

Prove safe interruption/retry of denial publication, MAC unloading and account
finalization, including actual admission denials while payload is absent and
the owned kiosk identity survives, on Ubuntu and Fedora.

## Selected work and explicit gaps

Select finite installed interruption points at denial publication, MAC policy
unloading and account finalization. Retain each interruption, refusal/recovery
and successful owned retry in one continuous history. Establish allowed controls
before denials and independently observe the enforcing boundary.

During the payload-erased, surviving-account interval, exercise actual kiosk
account/session admission denials through the durable distro PAM barriers and
prove ordinary-user access. Preserve ownership/recovery records and foreign
state, verify enforcement is restored before any guard release, and restore
exact original PAM bytes and local-versus-vendor disposition only after verified
owned-account deletion. A completed cleanup endpoint or private embedded-finalizer
fixture does not prove this interval. Keep the additional native RPM failed
post-erase/reinstall/reboot/repeated-purge chain in 4-6.

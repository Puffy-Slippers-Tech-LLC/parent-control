## Complete proof

Run the equivalent entire early-proof history through actual Fedora GDM and
enforcing SELinux: passwordless usable form, real parent approval, Cancel/exit/
reentry, unrelated-layout and cosmetic variations, and one bounded owned renewal.
Include the same unfamiliar PAM and automatically activated service fixtures
with effective ordinary-account controls, usable kiosk approval and exclusion or
confinement of the service's launch route. Validate form/authentication readiness
within the declared entry/recovery budget under the
[measurement contract](../SystemDesign/Lifecycle.md#one-measured-entry-and-recovery-budget).
Prove first exec, classify pre-boundary trusted policy helpers' vendor authority
and paired cleanup separately from confined kiosk manager/service/application
descendants, and require no child-reachable unconfined launch effect under the
[integrated proof](../SystemDesign/Kiosk-Confinement.md#finite-early-proof-and-implementation-limits).
Prove credential/session teardown and the preserved privileged Polkit helper;
do not assume an immediate transition of the multithreaded GDM worker.
Explicitly distinguish
unavailable approval from successful confinement; disabling privilege transitions
is not a solution.

Require working allowed controls before alternate-desktop/SSH and reachable
external-launch denials. Preserve ordinary account authentication, failed/cancelled
parent approval behavior, original errors and exact fixture restoration.
Use the canonical shared history; do not fork platform-specific scenario logic
when only a supporting OS adapter differs.

review/build and a comparison against R03's exact inputs. Repeat any invalidated
Ubuntu proof before closing this architecture gate; retain unaffected evidence
without claiming a new execution. No host-only or old root-group witness qualifies
the actual caller.

If the design fails either platform, resolve its bounded architectural cause
before R05. Do not revive broad inventory or special-case unknown host names.
Record both-target applicability and open limits. Session exception: keep this
installed history, recovery and restoration together.

Acceptance: installed
Targets: onpc-Ubuntu26.04

Estimate: 60–120 minutes; session exception for the complete early architecture proof.
Use the [R01 mechanism estimate](../SystemDesign/Kiosk-Confinement.md#implementation-estimates-after-integrated-review);

Requires R02. Read the [master](../Kiosk-Resilience-Plan.md#acceptance), accepted

## Complete proof

Run R02's exact maintained selector on the actual Ubuntu GDM/AppArmor path.
Verify passwordless usable child/parent selection, genuine parent approval,
Cancel/exit and successful reentry. Repeat after one unrelated host-layout
change and one missing cosmetic resource; exercise one bounded stale/failed
owned-instance renewal. Run the master's unfamiliar PAM and automatically
activated service fixtures, first proving their ordinary-account effects; usable
kiosk entry and genuine approval must coexist with excluding or confining the
service's launch route. Verify form/authentication readiness within the declared
entry/recovery budget under the [measurement contract](../SystemDesign/Lifecycle.md#one-measured-entry-and-recovery-budget).
Capture actual caller credentials and the original cause
of any rejected entry rather than accepting a transport timeout classification.

Prove the permitted actual first exec and classify pre-boundary trusted policy
helpers' vendor authority and paired cleanup separately from confined kiosk
manager/service/application descendants, with no child-reachable unconfined
launch effect, under the [integrated proof](../SystemDesign/Kiosk-Confinement.md#finite-early-proof-and-implementation-limits).
Then exercise alternate-desktop/SSH and
exposed launch-path controls. An ordinary child/admin account retains
authentication and access.
The form must remain usable after denied/cancelled parent authentication.
Use public customer operations for customer results and engineering probes only
for their separate boundary claims.

## Acceptance and close-out

All early-proof outcomes in the master must pass on one applicable candidate.
If the smaller architecture cannot meet them, retain the first failure and
return to a bounded design correction before broad implementation. Do not add
another ambient exception or waive a normal rejection as intended.

Any product correction follows the master's review/build rules and repeats the
affected full history after the final edit. Restore every owned fixture and
complete runner cleanup. Record candidate/input identities, public results and
proof limits for R04. Session exception: keep refusal/recovery/reentry and
restoration together; no independent clean-VM pass substitutes for this history.

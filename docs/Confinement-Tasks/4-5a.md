Acceptance: installed
Targets: onpc-Ubuntu26.04, onpc-Fedora44
Requires: 4-4

## Required result

Prove safe removal refusal while a root PAM admission or UID/exec handoff holds
the lifetime lease before kiosk credentials appear, on Ubuntu and Fedora.

## Selected work and explicit gaps

Exercise the installed root PAM admission/handoff boundary with a bounded held
lease, rather than relying on an already visible kiosk executor. Establish
successful native and ordinary-user controls before attempting removal. Require
refusal without signalling the holder or changing payload, PAM, ownership or
recovery records; verify enforcement remains effective.

Release only the attempt-owned holder through its normal lifetime, then complete
the same history's successful owned retry/finalization. Preserve all applicable
assertions of the maintained removal route. Active-manager refusal and
non-destructive check-remove preflights prove only their existing scope.

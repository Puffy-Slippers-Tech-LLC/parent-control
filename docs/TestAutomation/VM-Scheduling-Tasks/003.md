## Finite deliverable

- Expose one `execute_pass` coordinator path with optional repair actions for
  validated selections and saved pass identities. Return accepted outcomes,
  terminal failures, stopped/suspended state and repair/source-change status.
  Task 004 composes impact triage and deferred repair here; action callbacks
  cannot become a second scheduler or force every failure to stop admission.
  Keep the existing host resource scheduler; host work runs once per pass without
  VM slots and participates in the same source-admission boundary.
- Resolve the run's allowed [VM pool](../VM-Scheduling-Design.md#vm-pool-and-family-scheduling)
  from `--vm`, otherwise configured eligible VMs. Keep every included family in
  the pass, independently covering the full specified selection under existing
  applicability rules. Give cases separate pending/completed identities per
  family; an accepted case on one equivalent family VM completes that family's
  case only. A busy family waits without losing its required coverage.
- Balance active normal workers across families offering available VMs and
  pending work. Fill one per family before a second, up to concurrency, each
  family's available capacity and runnable work. On refill, prefer families with
  fewer active workers and rotate equal-count priority; do not preempt active attempts.
  Rotate admission when concurrency cannot represent every family, and use spare
  capacity when another family is busy or drained. Keep this finite selection
  rule here and delegate reservations/capacity to task 002's executor.
- Use `vm_case_queue.py` only as a planner in the replacement path. Leave shared
  legacy claim/resource helpers in place for their remaining repair callers;
  task 004 deletes them, completing the pure-planner boundary. Use one FIFO per
  family across selected categories per pass, preserving selector/reverse order,
  current preparation rules and explicit prerequisites without new category barriers.
  E2E cases, standalone checks, stateful histories and shared-preparation system
  areas keep their current indivisible grouping; prerequisites are not normal work.
- Ready VMs pull the next FIFO item on demand from their own family. The
  coordinator alone atomically records each claim before execution and writes
  scheduling state; prohibit overlapping selected case identities within a
  family and pass. Do not allocate case lists or fixed shards to VMs in advance.
  Equivalent workers execute one attempt at a time. Recovery and focused
  attempts remain on their required VM. Workers do not own another queue or
  advance categories, passes or rounds independently.
- Persist one logical run record with family partitions, claims and accepted
  completions using existing validated storage. Give pass, assignment, attempt and
  repair transaction distinct identities. Worker checkpoints/reports are bounded
  inputs to one acceptance path, not another family completion authority.
- Accept results only when run/pass/assignment/attempt/VM/original selection and
  source/input identity match, and final teardown/owned cleanup succeeds. Reject
  missing, stale, mismatched or diagnostic results. A late cleanup failure leaves
  work unfinished. Preserve partial passes only under the current checkpoint
  contract; recreate required lifecycle state and credit only actually qualified work.
- Resume under exclusive coordinator ownership: recover uncertain claims through
  their original workers before requeueing. Preserve valid accepted progress and
  selected totals under existing inventory/selector rules; crash recovery may
  repeat execution but cannot fabricate completion. Cleanup/reset use these same
  transitions under ownership, not an independent claim-rewriting algorithm.

[runner coverage](../../../tests/unit/test_regression.py) and

expect `2/2/2` active workers at concurrency six and `2/2/1` at five. Check fair
refill and eventual family admission below the family count, unequal/busy/drained
families, explicit-pool confinement and configured-pool fallback. Check ready-VM
FIFO claims with no advance sharding, one accepted execution per case per family
and no cross-family pass credit. These representative cases belong to existing
core scheduling coverage, not a new scheduler test matrix.

Check nonoverlapping family work, ordered/prerequisite grouping, host-once
execution, stale/partial/late-cleanup rejection, retained-pass resume and uncertain
claim recovery. Maintain representative core guarantees, not new helper tests.
Check remaining legacy repair callers before removing or changing shared APIs.
Review changed resource ownership and affected classifications; check Markdown.

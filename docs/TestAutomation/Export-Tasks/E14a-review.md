# E14a — Independently review final delivery

Acceptance: review
Requires: E14

## Purpose and delivery

Deliver the original E14 independent final-review obligation under the
[master](../Export-Execution-Plan.md) and
[infrastructure acceptance](../Export-Design.md#infrastructure-acceptance).
Read the E01b-frozen history/producer/consumer/retirement/dependency inventories,
delivered source boundaries, existing owning contracts and exact E14 results.
Apply the master's [mandate boundaries](../Export-Execution-Plan.md#delivery)
when assessing coverage: every family needs a disposition, while shared core
guarantees need focused direct checks and only distinct composition checks.
Flag redundant recursive tests and any UI tests of test tools; preserve unique
safety guarantees and product assertions. Historical counts or exhaustive
consumer/input combinations are not approval criteria.

Obtain GPT-6.1 Sol xHigh final review with a self-contained assignment and
`fork_turns="none"`. Verify exact identities and full proof, early capture,
bounded allocation/reuse, every retirement route, actual-file restart coverage,
claim-protecting advisory severity, legacy authorization and all consumer
dispositions. Inspect the required integration histories and their real call
paths; direct bundle reads or mocked change dictionaries cannot prove them.
Check that existing fixes were reused and no product acceptance, VM work,
competing archive state machine or broad privileged interface was introduced.

Resolve actionable findings and rerun only acceptance affected by material
revisions, within the master's host-only scope. Preserve exact prior results,
unrelated work and all ownership/session/authorization guards. Pending historical
proof or external protected application remains an explicit limit, never a pass.

## Validation and close-out

Retain the independent review and any corrective checks in normal launcher
evidence. Verify plan/help `--check`, changed links, source/build scope and resource
review completion. Confirm enduring delivered requirements have moved to their
existing owners and all frozen families have verified or explicitly pending
dispositions. Follow shared close-out for this final task; no other queue gains
completion credit and no missing product acceptance is executed.

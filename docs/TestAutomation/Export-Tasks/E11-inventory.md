# E11 — Inventory legacy references and recoverability

Acceptance: host
Requires: E10

## Purpose and delivery

Deliver deterministic read-only inventory under the
[master](../Export-Execution-Plan.md) and
[migration design](../Export-Design.md#compatibility-and-migration). Read the
frozen document/consumer list, delivered parser/resolver, accepted-evidence ledger,
historical recovery and authorized artifact-reading interfaces.

Scan tracked relevant documents plus explicitly registered local catalogues.
Classify exact live proof, exact archived proof, wrong/ambiguous destination,
unavailable history, example and operational path. Report consumer/line, claim
revision, required/optional/active role, original identity, verified candidate,
availability/refusal class and migration owner. Include ordinary plans beyond
kiosk-prompt and references in prose/briefs/handoffs. Do not perform an unbounded
filesystem search, mutate a live checkpoint, acquire a competing controller or
renew any product acceptance. Treat denied/untrusted sources distinctly from
missing. Emit only bounded local metadata through shared storage; no raw secret
or transcript dump and no new ad hoc evidence document.

## Validation and close-out

Follow the master's [mandate boundaries](../Export-Execution-Plan.md#delivery).
Reuse parser, identity and refusal tests; exercise the inventory's own distinct
classification/composition guarantees with representative inputs, including
stable ordering/digests, mixed syntax, nested documents, two
checkouts/targets, matching versus ambiguous archive members, unavailable output
on a clean checkout and preserved permission refusal. Run a read-only real
inventory through the registered interface and retain it in normal launcher
artifacts; report exact remaining categories without claiming recovered proof.
Run scoped host tests, update the maintained inventory/reader owner and follow
the master's cleanup and close-out.

## Repair cutover

Use task 003's allowed pool, balanced family admission and on-demand FIFO claims
for normal testing in `fix-tests`; add no repair-specific selection or sharding.
Every family retains its full selected coverage. The required-VM repair and
recovery attempts below preserve their recorded target within that pool.

1. Compose bounded impact-triage and repair actions into `execute_pass`.
   The ticket fixes original work/selectors, reported or attempted case, VM (empty
   for host work), failed attempt and stable transaction ID; never executable
   command text. Normal failure-stop keeps cooperative cancellation and
   continue-on-errors keeps its independent-work semantics. In repair mode,
   enqueue each failure before releasing source protection, initially holding
   new case admission as untriaged. Admitted histories/areas/cases finish through
   cleanup; additional failures join the observed FIFO. Collect the failed
   operation, expected/actual result, bounded logs and relevant shared inputs
   sufficient to judge impact. Do not wait for root cause or start a long
   diagnostic campaign before impact triage.
2. Run a fresh read-only impact-triage session using the selected adapter's
   logical high-effort profile: Codex `gpt-6.1-sol` with `high`; Claude Code
   Opus 5.5 with equivalent native high effort. Resolve the native model/effort
   in the adapter, not the scheduler; low unit-repair defaults cannot lower it.
   The Claude mapping is planned: current workflow qualification remains a
   prerequisite, and this task does not enable an unqualified adapter or silently
   switch providers. Reuse existing session supervision and capacity recovery
   without lowering the logical high-effort tier. Read-only triage holds a source
   reader lease; its own normal-case admission hold must not block that read.
   An active source writer still fences it. Impact holds do not prevent owned
   cleanup or the active ticket's authorized recovery/verification attempts.
   Return `yes`, `no` or `unsure` to whether the failure may affect another case,
   with a short reason and affected scope. Consider other families, host work,
   shared source, fixtures and prerequisites; `no` needs positive scoped evidence.
   Missing, malformed, stale, interrupted or unavailable triage remains `unsure`.
   Persist the decision against the ticket, failed attempt, source generation
   and relevant inputs. Changed evidence invalidates `no` until retriage or an
   accepted recheck resolves the failure. Impact triage grants no case pass.
3. For `yes` or `unsure`, keep new normal case admission closed across the
   participating checkout workflows while active readers drain, then repair.
   For `no`, remove only that ticket's impact hold and continue admitting other
   eligible normal cases through the existing family FIFOs. A nonempty repair
   FIFO alone must not block them. Triage pending failures promptly even when an
   earlier isolated repair is waiting. The coordinator owns decisions; the shared
   source guard exposes their durable admission holds without another queue or
   completion ledger. Gate changes and normal case admission are atomic.
   Repair edits still require the exclusive source lease. An isolated failure
   therefore waits for a naturally drained boundary after the current finite
   pass's eligible normal work finishes; do not close admission solely to obtain
   its writer lease or add checkout snapshots. A waiting isolated ticket must
   not publish writer intent that prevents new readers; claim the writer only
   at an atomically confirmed drained boundary. Settle repairs before starting
   another pass. A later blocking failure can force the drain sooner. Keep repair
   transactions in observed FIFO order; persist repair/Git continuation before
   side effects and keep supervision servicing active work, cancellation/reload.
4. Retain the failed VM's supervised reservation through investigation and
   close-out, without holding a slot while waiting. After validated attempt
   cleanup, an isolated ticket may leave that same owned worker serving other
   normal family cases until repair begins; retaining recovery identity must
   not idle the family's only VM or strand its FIFO. Scheduling locks cover only
   transitions, never waits for agents, resources, Git, subprocesses or cleanup.
   Recheck the FIFO head after preceding repairs through
   `execution.attempt(purpose, target=None)` before spending another agent turn.
   Recheck, diagnostic and verification attempts use the same coordinator,
   executor and validator, original VM/selectors and writer's borrowed source
   lease. Admit nested attempts only for that active fenced ticket. Never
   recursively launch a family scheduler.
5. Keep exact-case verification fresh and bounded, with only required
   predecessors. Missing/retired case identity uses the existing bounded
   replacement-selection rule within original selectors; case-based suites never
   widen to a category. Non-case leaves retain finite command verification.
   Diagnostics earn no completion. Verification must match current source/inputs;
   subsequent changes to those inputs require fresh qualification. Refresh
   content-qualified inputs for subsequent attempts without erasing historical passes.
6. Accept exactly `resolved`, `leave_failed` or `suspended` as repair outcomes,
   separately from the impact decision. Resolution references
   an accepted no-edit recheck, or fresh verification plus completed repair/Git
   close-out (including existing no-change close-out). Git/repair journals own
   their phases; the coordinator keeps references, not copied phase state.
   `leave_failed` retains failure without pass credit. It may settle an isolated
   ticket only after no unfinished writer/repair-owned edits remain; unresolved
   `yes`/`unsure` impact stops or suspends the run rather than reopening admission.
   `suspended`, exceptions, lost results and mismatched references retain the
   ticket, continuation and any source fence/impact hold. Resume recovers original
   VM ownership and finishes accepted Git work first, then restores triage and
   admission state; untriaged or invalid decisions default to `unsure`.
   Cancellation waits for all owned cleanup. Admission may reopen with isolated
   tickets still pending, but only when no blocking ticket or writer remains.

## Rounds, task accounting and removal

- `fix-tests` requests passes instead of assigning cases. Round 1 processes the
  selected categories and repairs; rounds 2 through X require a full clean pass.
  Any repair or admitted source change during verification requests another
  fresh complete pass of that same selection after repairs settle. Compare shared
  source generation at admission/finalization so independent participating runs'
  repairs count too. Bounded verification cannot finish a round. Explicit
  selectors never widen to `all`; unfiltered runs retain full regression scope.
  Terminal left-failed outcomes finish finitely as failure, never clean or looping.
- One run-wide round waits for every selected family and host work. Resume retains
  the current pass and its valid partial results; a requested new clean pass has
  a new identity and intentionally repeats its full selection.
- Use existing `TaskBudget` with stable logical admissions: a round-1 category or
  explicitly selected verification category covers all selected families; an
  unfiltered verification round is one composite task retained across fresh
  passes until clean completion or terminal failure. Fresh selected verification
  passes admit category tasks again. Exact-case attempts, family partitions and
  resumed close-out cost no additional tasks. The coordinator records admission
  once before first assignment and completion once when scope settles, including
  settled failure without pass credit.
  A limit blocks new tasks but lets admitted family work, repair and close-out
  finish. Resume reuses charged admissions. Impact triage counts once against
  the existing per-case agent-session allowance, including for unit failures;
  reuse its classification instead of adding a duplicate classification session
  for the same evidence. Retained accepted triage is not another session on
  resume. Keep subsequent repair escalation, intervention limits and Git policy.
- Show logical case progress by family/category in the current pass, one run-wide
  round/pass and VM assignments/attempts/resource waits. Preparation and replay
  never inflate totals; display never grants credit. No test-tool UI tests.
- Delete `fix_tests_queue.py` after moving repair actions and source protection to
  their owners. Remove repair family journals, category/round pipelines, VM slots,
  progress reconciliation, obsolete claim writers/recovery readers and the last
  legacy claim/resource helpers in `vm_case_queue.py`. No wrapper
  around mutable legacy scheduling state or dual-running compatibility path remains.
  Apply the version guard before public cutover; do not reinterpret old budgets.
- Update help and owning scheduling, resume, progress and task-budget contracts
  in `tests/README.md`, `tests/integration/README.md`, `docs/Execution-Plans.md`
  and applicable mandates in this task. State run-wide rounds and VM-independent
  counts and impact-based admission as deliberate changes; replace unconditional
  FIFO-wide admission stops and remove descriptions of independent pipelines.
  Document the adapter-resolved high-effort profile and its qualification gate.

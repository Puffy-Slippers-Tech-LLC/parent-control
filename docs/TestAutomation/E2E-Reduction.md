# Suggested test reductions

Review of all unfinished tasks in the [task list](E2E-Task-Queue.md).
The table contains remaining recommendations. The feedback retry-expiry journey
(task 259, case 217) was removed from the task list and inventory; focused retry
expiration checks remain, without a complete installed retry-deadline journey.

Keep strong coverage of children receiving the right amount of time, losing access
when it runs out, and being allowed or refused access to apps. Keep parental
approval, cancellation, ending approved access, separate children’s settings and work, and
restrictions surviving sign-out, sleep, restart and updates.
Keep checks that failed operations cannot accidentally give a child access.

The table identifies repeated journeys and lower-priority detail. Combining
journeys must preserve the result after each step, including unsuccessful
requests; checking only the final result would miss important failures.
Preparation still needed by a retained journey stays in place.

Snap and Flatpak are two supported ways of installing apps; the app checks
below keep coverage of both.

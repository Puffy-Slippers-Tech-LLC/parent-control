## Purpose and finite audit

Prove the final candidate satisfies the replacement contract on both platforms
and remove obsolete requirements without erasing historical work or inventing
acceptance. This task performs no VM execution by default.
Map actual final changed inputs to every A–H result, declared combination,
permitted/prohibited route pair and diagnostic recovery/download receipt.
Check effective authorization, confinement and original-cause privacy evidence,
including cleanup and restored owned state. Review all remaining entry rejection
predicates against their explicit ledger and concrete reachable risk or actual
essential setup/admin policy. Unknown configuration alone is never sufficient;
an unexplained normal positive refusal prevents closure.

## Reconciliation route

Read complete relevant source and callers where receipt applicability is unclear.
Update specification/design/inventory owners consistently with R01's decisions;
remove superseded code/tests/docs only with the delivered replacement identified.
Preserve historical results, case identities and retained runner/checkpoint
artifacts. Do not revive exact-generation machinery, a universal host allowlist
or basename-only restrictions as implicit acceptance obligations.
If final inputs invalidate evidence, retain the exact gap and insert a bounded
owned prerequisite through the shared plan contract for the affected complete
history. Keep R12 unchecked until it passes; reconciliation is not execution.

## Validation and close-out

Use maintained Markdown link and applicable host consistency checks; run affected
regressions and final `make build` if cleanup edits packaged inputs. Material
product revisions require the master's Astra review and localization gates and
new applicability review. Do not launch a broader product campaign by default.
Close only R12 after final both-platform evidence, rejection audit and cleanup
pass; record concise final evidence and set the master `Next task: none.`.

# Share VM workers with preparation

Acceptance: host

## Purpose and prerequisites

Give reservations and execution capacity one owner. Requires 001; trust its
completed boundary without renewing its validation.

Read the [master](../VM-Scheduling-Design.md),
[VM mandate](../../Mandates/VM-Mandate.MD), preparation's applicable
[environment contracts](../../../tests/integration/Environment.md#reusable-preparation-ownership)
and [storage mandate](../../Mandates/Test-Storage-Mandate.md).
Implementation routes: `tools/vm_queue.py`, `tools/vm_case_queue.py`,
`tools/vm_test_queue.py`, `tools/prepare_vm_host.py`,
`tools/prepare_appsnapshot.py` and their maintained public callers.

## Finite deliverable

- Make `vm_queue.py` the shared executor: open an identity-checked reserved
  supervised worker, execute one finite operation with capacity, then close or
  cancel through owned cleanup. These are internal responsibilities, not new CLI
  commands or an extensible operation-policy framework.
- Each independent worker owns one VM reservation; the parent holds none.
  Accept only canonical targets from the run's allowed
  [VM pool](../VM-Scheduling-Design.md#vm-pool-and-family-scheduling): explicit
  `--vm` selection, otherwise configured eligible VMs. Busy targets never
  authorize substitution outside that pool. Family balancing and case claims
  belong to task 003's coordinator, not a second policy in the executor.
  Separate availability/reservation from execution slots. Occupied-VM waiters
  consume no slot; explicit single-VM runs and preparation obey configured
  concurrency through this same path. Preserve its existing run-local scope and
  the host-wide VM pool/disk queue ordering and nested receipt borrowing.
- Move ordinary tests' resource admission here. Retain existing case-helper
  entry points still imported by legacy repair until task 004 removes their last
  caller; do not copy them or add an interim adapter. Request workers only for
  pending work eligible for admission or retained recovery; do not pre-reserve
  every allowed VM. A normal case is claimed only when its worker is ready.
  Cancel unused waiters after family work drains. A now-unneeded busy
  VM must not delay completion. Hold no reader lease during a VM wait and no
  execution slot during a source/impact-admission wait; recheck source protection
  after reservation. A foreign retained writer refuses and releases an idle reservation.
- Preparation uses a finite per-VM `run_each` convenience path in the executor,
  returning results by VM without another pool, semaphore or durable scheduler.
  Preserve confirmations, baseline/snapshot semantics, input checks and journals.
  Continue after an individual preparation failure. Cancellation prevents queued
  work, lets active baseline replacement finish, and retains snapshot cooperative
  cleanup. Refresh helpers once after all baseline reservations are released.
- Retain failed workers' reservations for future repair/recovery, releasing their
  execution slots while waiting. Events, timeouts and controller exit never prove
  cleanup. Keep SSH-host refusal and explicit host identities. Legacy repair
  scheduling remains isolated until task 004; add no interim resource adapter for it.

## Scoped validation and expected results

Select affected existing VM-pool and launcher integrity cases from
[VM-pool coverage](../../../tests/unit/test_vm_pool.py) and
[launcher coverage](../../../tests/unit/test_test_launchers.py). Through the
maintained unit runner, verify allowed-pool confinement, free-worker progress
beside an occupied VM, capacity on single/multiple selections, nested borrowing,
drained-family waiter cancellation, and reservation retention through owned
cleanup. Maintain bounded existing checks of preparation continuation,
cancellation and final helper refresh.
Confirm retained legacy repair imports/callers still work until their cutover.
Test ownership/capacity boundaries once with representative controlled operations;
do not build a consumer matrix. Record explicit selectors/timeouts when chosen.

Review changed resources in every applicable host scheduling inventory; check
Markdown links. Live preparation and installed execution belong to task 005.

## Cleanup and close-out

Join owned workers and children before releasing their receipts and allocations.
Preserve unfinished real VM journals; never seize a foreign reservation. Update
the VM/preparation owners with actual admission and cleanup behavior, record the
short scoped result and close this row through the shared plan contract.
